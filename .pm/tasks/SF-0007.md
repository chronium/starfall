Align Starfall's repository policy with the coordinator's automatic pointer-only handoff. The completed Starfall task remains the sole PM owner; after its focused child commit, the coordinator verifies linked identity, ancestry, and clean worktrees, then creates a pointer-only commit in the same approved cycle without a separate SUBMODULE task.

Acceptance criteria:
- Starfall AGENTS.md, source-control skill, and repository-workflow wiki remove the obsolete separate coordinator SUBMODULE task requirement.
- The child implementation and coordinator pointer remain two focused commits in their owning repositories.
- The coordinator follow-up records the canonical Starfall task URI, stable Starfall project ID, and pinned child commit.
- The workflow fails closed on identity, ancestry, dirty-tree, unexpected coordinator-change, or linked-project availability problems.
- Pushing remains owner-directed and ordered Starfall first, coordinator second.
- The Starfall task completes and commits before the coordinator gitlink is advanced automatically.
- No source, dependencies, runtime behavior, generated content, or unrelated PM/wiki scope changes.
- Starfall PM validation, skill validation, diff checks, family warnings, and repository/submodule state pass.
