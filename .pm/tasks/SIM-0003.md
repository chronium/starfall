Define deterministic camp definitions and replenishment policies for the three Draft 0 hunting spaces.

Acceptance criteria:
- Represent the easy broad/open, mixed elongated or divided, and hard tight/bowl camp shapes and placements from content.
- Define bounded capacity, initial population, replenishment timing, authoritative seeds, and valid ground-plane placement inputs.
- Preserve the contract: camp definition -> spawn/replenishment policy -> world-owned entities.
- Share deterministic definitions and policies with authoring and headless simulation while the world owns actual entities.
- Do not implement monster behavior, combat, ecology, a population service, or a generic spawning framework.
