Replace Basic Arrow's fixed-tick hitscan damage with a bounded deterministic authoritative straight-projectile lifecycle.

Acceptance criteria:
- Consume the approved Draft 0 straight-projectile Content input without depending on Protocol or presentation.
- At acceptance, validate actor/target/range/facing/stationary state, capture the target identity and current ground-plane position, freeze the actor release origin and normalized direction, and schedule release at start tick plus 18.
- During windup, never update or reacquire aim. After monster movement, release validation checks only that the actor remains alive/available at the frozen origin and the original target remains live; it does not recalculate aim, range or facing.
- Cancel when the actor becomes invalid or moves before release, or the original target dies/disappears before release. Once released, the projectile is independent of the original target, shooter and session.
- Advance projectile entities in ascending projectile-ID order through continuous swept ground-plane collision against currently live monster radii and approved static graybox collision.
- Resolve earliest contact; use ascending monster entity ID for equal monster contacts, static collision for exact static/monster ties, and contact over TravelExhausted exactly at 12 metres.
- Apply 300 integer damage only to the first contacted live monster. Static contact and 12-metre exhaustion apply no damage.
- Make each later projectile in the tick observe damage/death from earlier projectiles. Resolve projectile damage before monster attacks and discard an already-generated attack whose attacker was killed by a projectile.
- Permit a moving target to evade and another monster to intercept.
- Do not add homing, gravity, altitude, player/PvP collision, ammunition, a dynamic Box3D projectile body, Fire Arrow behavior, Arrow Rain or a generic projectile framework.
