Define the deterministic Draft 0 zone content and authoring inputs.

Acceptance criteria:
- Specify an approximately 200 x 200 metre map with an approximately 50 x 50 metre protected town near one edge and a configured respawn anchor.
- Give the town two or three landmark buildings without inventing NPC functionality.
- Define one readable exit leading to short, medium, and long branches, with experimental distances around 25, 45, and 70 metres.
- Define broad/open, elongated or divided, and tight/bowl camp spaces for easy, mixed, and hard hunting geometry.
- Record flat terrain, grass and dirt-path surface inputs, outer rock boundaries, branch separation, deterministic collision, and navigation inputs.
- Keep this focused on one zone; do not design a general world format, terrain engine, streaming system, biome system, or final art.
