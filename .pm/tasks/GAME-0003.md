Implement one bounded authoritative player inventory after an exact provisional item-input task is allocated during the future Inventory activation pass.

Acceptance criteria:
- Freeze one fixed provisional slot count and consume two or three exact development item definitions selected by that later content task.

- Keep mutable runtime state private and deterministic.
- Inventory simulation remains independently valid without ImGui, console injection, permanent GUI, physical drops or equipment.
- Do not implement nested or multiple containers, equipping, presentation, persistence, trading, crafting, economy or generalized item infrastructure.
