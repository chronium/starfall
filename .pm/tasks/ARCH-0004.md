Define and document Starfall's project, authority, service-availability, and failure-isolation boundaries before the repository and solution foundation is implemented.

## Core invariant

> Once admitted to a world, an active player's gameplay session must not depend on authentication, chat, or management services remaining available.

## Acceptance criteria

- Record logical ownership for identity/lobby, realm/world, chat, operations control plane, and persistence.
- Define authentication, roster/selection, short-lived signed world-join ticket issuance, ticket consumption, and creation of a world-owned gameplay session. Active gameplay performs no continuing identity-service authorization calls.
- Keep authoritative character rules and character data in the Starfall gameplay domain; allow the lobby only a read-optimized character-summary projection and orchestration of creation/selection requests.
- Treat each world/channel as an independent lifecycle and state owner so unrelated worlds can continue when one fails. Physical host granularity remains deferred.
- Keep combat, loot ownership, progression, equipment, and other gameplay-critical information on the game protocol. Chat routing and delivery are optional from gameplay's perspective.
- Define local-chat responsibility: the world may determine proximity recipients or publish presence information, while chat owns message routing, moderation, and delivery.
- Define the future operations boundary as an Angular client backed by an ASP.NET operations API. The browser never supervises processes directly, and the control plane is never on the gameplay hot path.
- Keep monster camps inside authoritative world simulation as: camp definition -> spawn/replenishment policy -> authoritative world entities. Authoring and the Balance Lab may share definitions and policies; no separate camp runtime service is introduced.
- Permit strict logical modules to share initial processes, possibly with identity/lobby and world as the only early executables. Do not equate a logical boundary with an immediate deployable.
- Preserve server authority and keep client presentation and headless dependency rules unchanged.
- Record persistence degradation as an unresolved contract with explicit later questions for admission, buffering/journaling, restricted durable operations, draining, recovery, and consistency.
- State the evidence required before selecting final process, deployment, and extraction topology.

## Non-goals

Do not implement services, service hosts, Angular or ASP.NET operations infrastructure, persistence, chat delivery, process supervision, distributed transactions, a population director, or a microservice-per-concept topology.
